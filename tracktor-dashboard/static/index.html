Operational Guidelines

Customer Service
  • Always explain cost estimates thoroughly before initiating repairs.
  • Share photos/videos via WhatsApp for major internal engine parts replacements.
  • Clearly commit to and document expected delivery dates.
Workshop Safety
  • Mechanics must wear welding masks and heavy gloves during welding works.
  • Always disconnect tractor batteries before carrying out any electrical system repairs.
  • Store fuels and lubricants in specialized cabinets away from the welding area.
Record Keeping
  • Initialize a formal Job Card for every single tractor entry.
  • Record cash, UPI, and outstanding dues transactions immediately.
  • Update the active repair status daily.
